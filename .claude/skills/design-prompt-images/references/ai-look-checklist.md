# AI-look checklist — review every generated image

View at **100% zoom**. Check **faces and hands first** — in a 2025 perception study they were ~62%
of the cues people used, and "too perfect" was the most common verdict. Categories follow Kamali et
al. 2024 (anatomical, stylistic, functional, physics, sociocultural).

**One fail → edit** (inpaint or a targeted re-prompt) **or discard. Two or more anatomy or physics
fails → re-roll.**

## Anatomy
- [ ] Finger count; no merged or missing nails; wrist and hand proportions right
- [ ] Teeth separate from lips; eyes not empty or glassy; limbs connect
- [ ] Earrings match; glasses have two arms

## Skin and hair
- [ ] No waxy, glossy or airbrushed skin; pores and small flaws visible
- [ ] Hair has strands, not a soft helmet
- [ ] No identical "model" faces, no stock smile at the camera

## Text and signage
- [ ] **No text at all** (the rule is a text-free plate) — check shopfronts, plates, phone screens,
      papers. Arabic pseudo-letters are especially common; any glyph is a fail.

## Brands
- [ ] No pseudo-badges on grilles, no half-Apple camera bump, no near-miss logos or sponsor strips

## Vehicles
- [ ] Wheels match; door seams and both mirrors present
- [ ] **Left-hand-drive car, traffic on the right** (Egypt); steering wheel on the left
- [ ] Reflections show the scene that is actually there

## Physics
- [ ] One light source direction; shadows agree; contact shadows under tyres and feet
- [ ] Nothing floating; reflections match

## Architecture
- [ ] Balconies and railings intact; windows line up floor to floor
- [ ] No repeating tile or brick pattern; no impossible stairs

## Functional
- [ ] Straps don't pass through bodies; buttons fasten; phones and keys are actually held

## Stylistic
- [ ] No HDR halos or oversaturation; not perfectly symmetrical
- [ ] Not everything in focus; bokeh not uniformly smooth; no painterly "render" sheen

## Sociocultural (Egypt)
- [ ] Looks like Egypt — not Gulf, not Europe (`egypt-realism.md`)
- [ ] Dress and modesty fit the campaign and season (higher in Ramadan)
- [ ] No clichés — pyramids, camels, desert for everyday commerce

## Layout
- [ ] The copy area is genuinely empty
- [ ] The subject survives both crops: desktop 1280×180 and mobile 390×150
- [ ] The RTL plate exists and is checked (not a flipped photo with cars or traffic)

## Legal
- [ ] No identifiable real person, no real place signage, no trademark, no legible plate
- [ ] Provenance recorded; labelled if it could be read as real
