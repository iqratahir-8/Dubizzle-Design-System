# Roles

Only roles that change what a screen shows belong here. Add one when a design differs by it.

- `signed-out` — a visitor. Sees Login; contact actions prompt to log in.
- `signed-in` — an individual seller/buyer with an account (My Ads, Chats, Favourites, Settings).
- `agency` — a dubizzle Pro portal user. **Desktop only** (D-012); has no mobile layout.

_Not yet confirmed with the product owner: whether there are more (moderator, business seller) and which
screens differ by role. Add them in `registry.json` as `role_differs`._
