Settings icons

Twelve icons for the account Settings area. Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, shown at 20px in currentColor, the same family as the live account menu. 5 reuse house icons; 7 are new (Apache-2.0).

Unverified rows. Settings entry, Privacy, Notifications, Change language, Help and Logout come from the live account menu and the captured routes. The rows inside the tabs, and the whole Manage account section, are from memory of the live page and have not been checked against a capture. Confirm them before these ship.

Each choice, and what was rejected

Settings

project:action/settings

Reused, found in the registry first. House icon (account menu 'Settings'). Material Symbols Rounded 400 at 20px; matches the set.

Privacy

material-symbols:shield-person-outline-rounded

Privacy here means who sees your personal details (the 'show my phone number' toggle). Shield-with-person says personal data is protected. privacy-tip reads as an info tip; shield-lock and lock read as security/password, and the lock is already used for protected-info.

Rejected

Show my phone number in ads

project:action/phone-number

Reused, found in the registry first. House icon; it is Material Symbols 'call' rounded, so it already matches. Used for the 'Show my phone number in ads' toggle.

Notifications

material-symbols:notifications-outline-rounded

Same bell as the house action/user-notifications, but at weight 400. The house file is weight 300 with a baked #464C55 fill, so beside the other 400-weight settings icons it reads lighter, as if disabled. action/notifications is a 1024-grid legacy glyph.

Rejected

Recommendations

material-symbols:thumb-up-outline-rounded

The toggle is 'recommendations based on your activity'. Sparkles are dubizzle's AI mark (misc/sparkle, gemini-stars), so they would promise AI. recommend-outline is a solid disc, the heaviest glyph in the list. Lightbulb means tips (listing-tip).

Rejected

Special communications & offers

material-symbols:campaign-outline-rounded

The toggle covers communications as well as offers: updates, surveys, promotions. A megaphone covers both. A price tag reads as Sell/price on a marketplace; gift and loyalty-heart promise rewards that do not exist.

Rejected

Manage account

material-symbols:manage-accounts-outline-rounded

Same glyph as the house user/manage-accounts at weight 400; the house file is drawn heavier (about 600) and is the darkest icon in the row.

Rejected

Log out of all devices

material-symbols:devices-outline-rounded

The row label carries the verb; the icon says 'all your devices'. Re-using the logout arrow would make it look like a duplicate of Logout. A crossed-out glyph reads as 'disabled'.

Rejected

Delete account

material-symbols:person-remove-outline-rounded

Removes you, not a thing. The bin is already 'delete ad' (action/delete), so it would make deleting an account look as light as deleting a listing. no-accounts and person-off read as blocked/banned.

Rejected

Change language

project:misc/language

Reused, found in the registry first. House icon (account menu 'Change language'). Translate glyph, kept rather than a globe so it stays the one the live menu ships.

Help & Support

project:misc/help

Reused, found in the registry first. House icon (account menu 'Help & Support').

Logout

project:brand/logout

Reused, found in the registry first. House icon (account menu 'Logout').
